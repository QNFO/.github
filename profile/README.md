QNFO is the personal research organization of [Rowan Brad Quni-Gudzinas](https://orcid.org/0009-0002-4317-5604). It is the umbrella for multiple interconnected programs spanning quantum computing, artificial intelligence, fundamental physics, and the philosophy of science.

**[qnfo.org](https://qnfo.org)** — The primary website, hosted via Obsidian Publish, containing the full research corpus: 414 releases, notes, blog posts, and archives dating back to 2024.

## Flagship Program: QWAV

**[QWAV](https://qwav.tech)** — Ultrametric Quantum Computing & AI. Passive fault tolerance at 4K via Bruhat-Tits tree architectures. Glass-box AI on tree topologies. One mathematical correction — replacing continuous geometry with ultrametric tree geometry — addresses two multi-billion-dollar problems.

### Key QWAV Results
- **Zero logical errors at depth 7** — ternary tree encoding, validated at physical error rates up to 40%
- **48x error reduction** — at zero additional qubit cost via q-ary scatter
- **Glass-box AI beats transformers** — 6.6x on hierarchical classification, 100% verification detection
- **40-atom neutral atom hardware specification** — within demonstrated experimental capabilities
