$$\frac{d\alpha_k}{dt} = i[H, \alpha_k]$$

$$[H, \alpha_k] = [\vec{\alpha} \cdot \vec{p} + \beta m, \alpha_k] = \beta m[\beta, \alpha_k] = -2i\beta\Sigma_{kj}p_j$$

$$\frac{d\alpha_k}{dt} = 2\beta\Sigma_{kj}p_j$$

$$\frac{d\alpha_k}{dt} = 0$$
